Privacy

one24 is run by Zach Koncir through 2625180 Ontario Inc. This page covers this website and the small tools one24 uses to publish and measure its own content. Last updated October 14, 2026.

This website

one24.ai sets no cookies and has no ads or trackers. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without fingerprinting visitors, so I see totals like visits, pages and referring sites, not individuals. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you pick a light or dark theme, your browser remembers that choice locally. It never leaves your device.

Social media tools

one24 uses its own private tool to publish posts to its LinkedIn accounts and to read how those posts perform. It only accesses accounts that belong to one24, and only to publish and measure one24’s own content. Post statistics are stored in aggregate on Cloudflare. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Email

If you email me, I keep the message to reply and for my own records. I don’t add you to any list.

Your choices

To ask what one24 holds about you, or to have it deleted, email zach@one24.ai.
