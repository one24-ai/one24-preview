About

I’m Zach Koncir, a principal engineer with nearly 20 years in infrastructure and systems. I came up through network operations and DevOps, led an AWS consulting practice, and now work on a platform that runs some of the world’s most-played online games. Lately my work centres on one question: how do engineers stay in charge as AI agents take on more of the work?

How I work

I care about the unglamorous parts of infrastructure: clear interfaces, safe defaults, and making the right thing the easy thing for the engineers who build on what I make.

The same idea has run through my work for years, from “freedom within boundaries” at DevOpsDays in 2019 to the guardrails in pi-governor today: give engineers room to move fast, and put the limits where they protect everyone. AI agents haven’t changed that. They’ve raised the stakes. An agent is just another user of your platform, and it deserves the same well-paved paths and the same firm boundaries.

Why one24

For most of my life I raced motocross, and 124 was the number on my plate. Years later the number came back: my first daughter was born on December 4, 12/4. So one24 is two things I care about, neither of which has anything to do with software, which is sort of the point.
